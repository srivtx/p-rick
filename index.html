The Green’s Function of Context: Positional Recall as an Inverse Design Problem

Context rot has an object: the impulse response of the positional channel. The recall profile is the kernel smeared by noise (0.037); the lost-in-the-middle dip lands exactly on its closed form (2771 = 2771); windows collapse with load exactly as the extreme-value law computes; and the monotonicity theorem says uniform recall is impossible with timescales alone — it takes a register. The register-plus-slope design prices the trade between uniform recall and order resolution by the decade.

P-027

The Dissipation Budget Law: Port-Hamiltonian Streams and the Conservation Architecture for Depth

Depth is rented, never owned. The port-Hamiltonian stream replaces the residual update with Cayley transport (exactly orthogonal — volume-free to 10^-14), metered dissipation (the only contraction channel: expressivity becomes a budget line), and ports as the only norm injection. Zero bound violations in 7,200 runs while the control explodes to 10^43; gradients metered through 240 layers; a rank-2 readout at depth 120 at 98.1% against the rescued control’s 38.3%, held-out over three seeds — and E6 trains it end-to-end: baseline parity at depth 240 with no normalizer anywhere (97.3% ± 0.3).

The charter
method · four tests

Research first, products later. Every paper verifies its gap, grades its evidence, and regenerates its figures from code — or it does not ship.

The bar

four tests, all mandatory The method →
test 01

Gap verified

Before anything is proposed, the landscape is searched for incumbents — products, papers, standards. A gap only counts if the search comes back empty.

test 02

Large audience

The missing thing must matter to many: a category with mass use, not a niche tool or a wrapper around an existing capability.

test 03

No toy output

Excluded by charter: test suites, deterministic wrappers, anything an AI obsolete. What ships is theory, architecture, or a validated harness.

test 04

Honest grading

Every paper grades its own evidence — claims are marked verified, estimation, or verification-queued. Constants are derived laws or labeled configuration-specific.

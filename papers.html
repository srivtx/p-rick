Papers

Sixteen working papers in four series. Each verifies its gap against the existing landscape before proposing anything, states the gap as a falsifiable claim, grades the evidence honestly (STRONG / PARTIAL / WEAK), and confronts the strongest objections to its own thesis. PDFs are hosted on this site; markdown sources live in the repository. The method page documents the bar and the exclusions; the citing block is at the bottom.

series IV · assumptions — the quiet premises software runs on · 2026
P-012

The Complexity Ledger: Design as Relocation, Not Reduction

theorydesignmeasurement

Every discipline that moves matter has an accounting discipline; the one that moves complexity has folklore. Tesler's law of conservation of complexity — fifty years quoted, never once operationalized — becomes a falsifiable theory over five sinks with field-measurable proxies (code, interaction, operation, documentation, deferral), double-entry relocation records that refuse one-legged claims of reduction, complexity budgets with reconciliation at release cadence, and a consent column separating trades from externalities. The waterbed signatures in compiler flags, ticket-migration signatures after UI "simplifications," and documentation-volume compensation after API redesigns are the theory's testable predictions.

P-013

Delegated Operation: Software for the Body with More Than One Owner

HCIsecurityaging

The single-principal assumption — one account, one human, present and able — fails the 53 million unpaid family caregivers who operate a parent's phone, bank, pharmacy, and email through shared passwords and video-call screen shares that security teams classify as abuse. We specify the operator principal: capability bundles instead of credential handover, a graduated autonomy ladder (observe → advise → co-act → act → break-glass), grants that are purpose-scoped, time-boxed, and device-bound, audit trails the primary owns, drift detection aimed at the operator, and succession semantics ending in an estate mode rather than a suspension email.

P-014

Modality Failover: Cross-Sensory Redundancy for Critical Software

accessibilityalertssystems

Alerting's hidden single point of failure is the human sensory channel: the deaf user's intact smoke alarm, the pocketed phone at 3 a.m., the clinical alarm-fatigue catastrophe — channel death treated as the environment's problem. We specify RAID for the senses: platform-granted criticality classes (advisory / action / life-safety), a local private capability profile of live channels, k-of-n delivery, acknowledgment-gated escalation across modalities, devices, and finally people, channel-health probing, and an antifatigue governor. CAP standardized the message; this is the missing receiving layer.

P-015

The Uncertain Document: Propagating Uncertainty Through Everyday Software

datastatisticsdocuments

Metrology solved error propagation a century ago (GUM); the office, the hospital, and the ministry still run on bare floats whose point-estimate form made the austerity-spreadsheet error invisible. We specify the quant — {point, distribution, provenance, confidence, units} — as the document's native numeric type, with tiered propagation (interval arithmetic, Monte Carlo, GUM's law), a calibrated display contract built on the natural-frequencies evidence, an assumption registry that ages, uncertainty that survives copy-paste and APIs, and the calibration ledger: the first instrument that lets an organization learn its own estimation bias.

P-016

The Defaults Ledger: Governing the Strongest Force in Software

governanceplatformsbehavioral econ

Defaults produce the largest measured effect sizes in behavioral science — organ-donation consent from 4% to 99% between neighboring countries, a $20B/year market price for one search box's placement — and the act of setting one is recorded nowhere. We specify the civic layer: machine-readable defaults manifests with provenance and changed-from, update-time diff and drift notification, a portable personal override registry, household and third-party negotiation layers (importable default packs), default impact assessments above exposure thresholds, and procurement-grade defaults bills of materials. Defaults are legislation nobody voted on; this is the parliamentary record.

series III · continuity — what systems lose over time · 2026
P-008

The Afterlife of Devices: An Architecture for Vendor-Death Resilience

IoTlifecycleescrow

Connected devices have physical lifetimes of a decade and corporate lifetimes of a few years, and the crossing is undesigned: Insteon darkened hubs overnight in 2023, Revolv was bricked on a published schedule in 2016. We specify succession-ready devices — a lifecycle state machine with an explicit succession transition, a device succession manifest, escrowed continuity released by verifiable dead-hand triggers (bankruptcy filings, certificate expiry, renewal silence), a threshold key-succession ceremony, a degradation ladder, and a hardware-guaranteed fossil mode. Every component exists in an adjacent industry; the composition does not exist anywhere.

P-009

Circadian Orchestration: Team Scheduling on Human Biological Time

HCI × ORchronobiologyprivacy

Collaborative calendars model availability — free or busy — and nothing else, while human performance varies by circadian phase with a population spread of four to six hours. We specify circadian orchestration: a private phase state (questionnaire-grade estimation, optional wearable calibration), circadian cost functions from the two-process model of sleep regulation, fairness-constrained meeting placement with minimax burden bounds, and the golden overlap — all under a privacy-first architecture where only binned cost surfaces leave the client and no individual's phase is ever transmitted. The evidence grades PARTIAL at components, STRONG at composition.

P-010

The Bus Factor Protocol: Succession Governance for Open-Source Package Registries

supply chaingovernanceregistries

Registries govern code identity — names, signing, provenance — but not maintainership continuity, so the supply chain's load-bearing packages decay into abandonment by default, and every catastrophic governance failure of the last decade began in the succession gap: left-pad, event-stream, colors, and the 2024 xz-utils backdoor, which was a succession attack executed through a tired maintainer's private handover. We specify the bus factor protocol: an abandonment state machine, succession policy as code, a dormancy escalation ladder ending in a narrow security-hold, an anti-xz trust-elevation ladder (co-signed, delay-locked early releases), dead-hand transfers, community takeover with tenure-in-graph vouching, and a public continuity ledger.

P-011

Model Extinction: Behavioral Conservation for the Age of Disposable AI

AI infraconservationreproducibility

We preserve web pages, source code, films, and seeds — but not the behavior of deployed AI models, the fastest-dying artifact class in software history. Same-name drift, fine-tune orphaning, unreproducible science, and silent product breakage are one uncounted extinction event. We specify behavioral conservation: the behavioral fingerprint (stratified, anchor-linked eval suites with confidence regions, including consumer-contributed regression strata), equivalence testing for replacements (TOST-based deprecation audits), an IUCN-style extinction registry, inference-time provenance tokens, archival access tiers, and enforceable deprecation contracts. Conserve the observable, not the substrate.

series II · systems · 2026
P-004

Degradation Contracts: Specifying and Enforcing How Software Behaves Under Pressure

systemsformal modelQoS

Every piece of software has two behaviors: the one it exhibits when resources are abundant, and the one under pressure. The first is engineered — specified, tested, monitored, budgeted. The second is emergent: accidental, untested, invisible. We propose degradation contracts: a declarative formalism (fidelity ladders per feature, safe shedding orders, hysteresis and recovery semantics, floor invariants) and a pressure broker that measures pressure, mediates directives, and audits compliance into a queryable ledger. The payoffs compound in the AI era, where quality is already a runtime variable negotiated invisibly; contracts make that negotiation inspectable and fair.

P-005

Provenance-Native Storage: The File System as a Causal Ledger

systemsstoragetrust

Every file is an amnesiac: it knows its size and mtime, and has forgotten who created it, from what inputs, over which network origin. PASS proved automatic file provenance feasible in 2006; nothing shipped. The barriers have expired: eBPF/EndpointSecurity/ETW capture substrates, content-addressed compaction, local analytical engines, and C2PA's market education. This paper specifies the causal event ledger, the application transform contract, the query algebra, and the retention semantics that treat forgetting as a feature — the trust infrastructure the AI era just made mandatory.

P-006

The Attention Scheduler: Interruption Economics as an Operating System Problem

OSHCI × systemsagents

OSes schedule processors, memory, and network with fifty years of theory; attention is scheduled by a DND toggle and an adversarial engagement economy at a measured cost of ~23 minutes of resumption per interruption, unrecorded. We specify the attention scheduler: interruption-cost models, admission control with deferral queues, breakpoint-timed dispatch, per-app budgets with honesty pricing, urgent-class bounded latency, starvation freedom, and an attention ledger. Closed platform features are approaching this system without the theory; the paper supplies the semantics first.

P-007

The Intermittent Compute Fabric: One Machine Across Many Devices

distributedlocal-firstAI infra

The median user owns five to eight devices that behave as islands joined only by cloud meters. The offloading research line died in 2012 for reasons that have quietly expired: mesh VPNs solved reachability, WASM and containers solved portability, device identity is solved, and distributed local AI is the first workload in a generation that needs cross-device placement. We specify the fabric: a scheduler that treats sleep and partition as inputs, a capability-delegation protocol, partition-tolerant task semantics, and an ambient CRDT state layer — one machine across many devices, owned.

series I · user-owned data & the economics of inattention · 2026
P-001

The Personal Event Bus: User-Owned Middleware for Ambient Digital-Life Events

middlewareprivacyevents

Phones receive the most valuable structured data of a person's life — payment events, receipts, gate changes, renewals — and throw it away before it can be kept. The personal event bus is user-owned middleware that captures ambient events, stores them under user control, and projects permissioned views to consuming applications: the missing skeleton of personal data infrastructure, specified formally.

P-002

Consumer Entitlements as Dead Capital

economicsrightsagents

Billions per year in statutory and contractual rights — compensation regimes, refund windows, class settlements — go unclaimed because rights are invisible, perishable, and unpriced. The personal entitlement engine treats a right as a machine-readable object with detection, valuation, and execution semantics, converting dead capital to live capital for the holder.

P-003

The n=1 Cost-of-Living Index

methodologyeconomicsstatistics

Official inflation is an average over a nation that matches no individual. This paper specifies the methodology for a statistically defensible personal price index — matched-model chaining, quality adjustment for shrinkflation, Bayesian shrinkage to official indices, uncertainty bands — and the local-first multi-source software that would compute it from a person's own receipts.

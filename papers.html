Papers

Twenty working papers in five series. Each verifies its gap against the existing landscape before proposing anything, states the gap as a falsifiable claim, grades the evidence honestly (STRONG / PARTIAL / WEAK), and confronts the strongest objections to its own thesis. PDFs are hosted on this site; markdown sources live in the repository. The method page documents the bar and the exclusions; the citing block is at the bottom.

series VII · collapse — critical thresholds where everyday infrastructure fails abruptly · 2026
P-024

Resolution Collapse: The Satisfiability Threshold of Version-Constrained Dependency Resolution

package managementCSPphase transition

Dependency resolution is a constraint-satisfaction problem whose density has been rising for two decades, and its failure is a phase transition: below a critical dependency density resolutions exist; above it they cease to exist at all. The first-moment bound Dc ≤ ln K / ln(K/w) (verified exact by brute-force enumeration on the cyclic version space), a measured gap law γ ≈ 0.71 with the constant labeled configuration-specific, the easy–hard–easy cost anatomy, the hardness tax that separates practical budgets from generous ones, the proliferation paradox (version growth at fixed range width is fragility; at proportional width, slow slack), and a growth experiment where crisis-pruning feedback alone pins an ecosystem just below its collapse threshold while the no-feedback arm crosses and installs die permanently. Solver validated against brute force; every figure regenerates from one file.

P-025

The Collapse Law: Ceilings, Hysteresis, and the Recovery Threshold for Timeout-Retry Systems

queueingSREclosed-form

Congestion collapse, priced. The good steady state of a timeout-retry system dies at a tangency with a closed form — λc = μθρ*²/(1+θρ*) from the timeout kernel alone, independent of every retry policy — and the collapsed state survives on the retry budget itself, dying only below λr ≈ μ/R, with drains measured in the thousands of service-times. Between the two: hysteresis, the 70%-shed-or-restart of every metastable postmortem. The knobs priced: jitter buys the tight-timeout margin back (0.81 of ceiling vs 0.50 for instant retry), deterministic backoff resonates (−36%), and admission caps work only inside the timeout horizon.

P-026

The Cascade Law of Credential Reuse: Channels, Concentration, and the Limits of Herd Protection

securitycascadespercolation

Every breach is every breach, and now that has arithmetic. An exact blast-radius law for the reuse channel (validated to 0.2%), an exact two-channel decomposition F = Fw + (1−λ)Fs, and a concentration law with a separatrix at password-popularity exponent 1: diverse pools make the cascade dilute (risk grows with the user base), concentrated pools — where real corpora sit — pin it at the backbone from the first breach onward. The defense asymmetry the folklore blurs: lockout friction is the only herd-level lever on the backbone; manager adoption is perfect private protection whose herd threshold is 99.997%. Rate-limit everything that sprays; hand every user a manager; know which is which.

The Reproduction Number of Code: Epidemic Thresholds for Dependency-Borne Compromise

securitynetworksepidemiology

The program’s quantitative turn: compromised packages as epidemics on the dependency DAG. The spectral threshold everyone computes on registries is exactly zero (nilpotence — it certifies nothing), the correct threshold is the branching law R₀ʲ = T·⟨d⟩, heavy-tailed registries sit in the thresholdless regime where the safe transmissibility zone vanishes with registry size, cascade-aware pinning buys herd immunity at one-eighth the random budget, and the registry yank has a half-life of two cascade steps. Derived, validated across 28 configurations, reproducible from one file.

P-022

The Admission Law: A Closed-Form Threshold for Cache Admission under Zipf Demand with Transients

cachingperformanceclosed-form

The folklore of admission filters (TinyLFU doors, probation segments), written as arithmetic: τ* = (1−f)W / (H·Bα) — the threshold tracks the stable-evidence volume, flat demand demands severe doors, and the door legally disables itself when capacity covers the head. The capacity tax prices the unfiltered cache at f/(1−f) of its memory; the law’s argmax matches the closed form within 1% where the choice matters, and gains over LRU reach 137% at the flat-and-dirty corner. Four measurements, one line of arithmetic, the door.

P-023

Generation Loss: A Drift Law for Continually Re-embedded Vector Corpora

AI-infraretrievaldriftrev 1.1

Every vector index goes stale between encoder upgrades, and nobody has the law: D(g) = 1−λg with λ the product of an isometry channel (dimension-mercied into harmlessness and removable exactly by a Procrustes landmark panel) and a distortion channel (dimension-free, un-alignable, the entire real cost of churn). Validated to three decimals; anchored stale vectors hold nearly double the recall at a rounding error of re-embedding cost. Revision 1.1: the draft's “L ≈ 8d” landmark constant is withdrawn and replaced by a derived estimation theorem — the panel budget is a four-factor law (accumulated distortion, panel coverage, estimator class, tolerance) whose knee-migration experiments (F6/F7) track within 11%; an undersized panel is shown to be worse than no panel at all. The re-embed calendar, replaced by two probes and one matrix solve.

The Revocation Protocol: Consent Withdrawal as a Propagation Guarantee

privacyprotocolsconsent

“You can withdraw anytime” is the most repeated promise in software, and withdrawal is a local event with no propagation semantics: the token dies at the first hop while the data already exported, resold, and folded into models keeps working. Grant objects with lineage, revocation events with coverage semantics, signed receipts, re-attestation cycles that make consent re-earned rather than remembered, canary audits, and a liability ladder where silence becomes evidence — the certificate ecosystem’s short-lived-cert lesson, applied to the largest class of unkept promises in software.

P-012

The Complexity Ledger: Design as Relocation, Not Reduction

theorydesignmeasurement

Every discipline that moves matter has an accounting discipline; the one that moves complexity has folklore. Tesler's law of conservation of complexity — fifty years quoted, never once operationalized — becomes a falsifiable theory over five sinks with field-measurable proxies (code, interaction, operation, documentation, deferral), double-entry relocation records that refuse one-legged claims of reduction, complexity budgets with reconciliation at release cadence, and a consent column separating trades from externalities. The waterbed signatures in compiler flags, ticket-migration signatures after UI "simplifications," and documentation-volume compensation after API redesigns are the theory's testable predictions.

P-013

Delegated Operation: Software for the Body with More Than One Owner

HCIsecurityaging

The single-principal assumption — one account, one human, present and able — fails the 53 million unpaid family caregivers who operate a parent's phone, bank, pharmacy, and email through shared passwords and video-call screen shares that security teams classify as abuse. We specify the operator principal: capability bundles instead of credential handover, a graduated autonomy ladder (observe → advise → co-act → act → break-glass), grants that are purpose-scoped, time-boxed, and device-bound, audit trails the primary owns, drift detection aimed at the operator, and succession semantics ending in an estate mode rather than a suspension email.

accessibilityalertssystems

Alerting's hidden single point of failure is the human sensory channel: the deaf user's intact smoke alarm, the pocketed phone at 3 a.m., the clinical alarm-fatigue catastrophe — channel death treated as the environment's problem. CAP standardized the message; this is the missing receiving layer.

The Uncertain Document: Propagating Uncertainty Through Everyday Software

datastatisticsdocuments

Metrology solved error propagation a century ago (GUM); the office, the hospital, and the ministry still run on bare floats whose point-estimate form made the austerity-spreadsheet error invisible. governanceplatformsbehavioral econ

Defaults produce the largest measured effect sizes in behavioral science — organ-donation consent from 4% to 99% between neighboring countries, a $20B/year market price for one search box's placement — and the act of setting one is recorded nowhere. The Afterlife of Devices: An Architecture for Vendor-Death Resilience

IoTlifecycleescrow

Connected devices have physical lifetimes of a decade and corporate lifetimes of a few years, and the crossing is undesigned: Insteon darkened hubs overnight in 2023, Revolv was bricked on a published schedule in 2016. We specify succession-ready devices — a lifecycle state machine with an explicit succession transition, a device succession manifest, escrowed continuity released by verifiable dead-hand triggers (bankruptcy filings, certificate expiry, renewal silence), a threshold key-succession ceremony, a degradation ladder, and a hardware-guaranteed fossil mode. Every component exists in an adjacent industry; the composition does not exist anywhere.

HCI × ORchronobiologyprivacy

Collaborative calendars model availability — free or busy — and nothing else, while human performance varies by circadian phase with a population spread of four to six hours. The Bus Factor Protocol: Succession Governance for Open-Source Package Registries

supply chaingovernanceregistries

Registries govern code identity — names, signing, provenance — but not maintainership continuity, so the supply chain's load-bearing packages decay into abandonment by default, and every catastrophic governance failure of the last decade began in the succession gap: left-pad, event-stream, colors, and the 2024 xz-utils backdoor, which was a succession attack executed through a tired maintainer's private handover. P-011

Model Extinction: Behavioral Conservation for the Age of Disposable AI

AI infraconservationreproducibility

We preserve web pages, source code, films, and seeds — but not the behavior of deployed AI models, the fastest-dying artifact class in software history. Same-name drift, fine-tune orphaning, unreproducible science, and silent product breakage are one uncounted extinction event. We specify behavioral conservation: the behavioral fingerprint (stratified, anchor-linked eval suites with confidence regions, including consumer-contributed regression strata), equivalence testing for replacements (TOST-based deprecation audits), an IUCN-style extinction registry, inference-time provenance tokens, archival access tiers, and enforceable deprecation contracts. Conserve the observable, not the substrate.

P-004

Degradation Contracts: Specifying and Enforcing How Software Behaves Under Pressure

systemsformal modelQoS

Every piece of software has two behaviors: the one it exhibits when resources are abundant, and the one under pressure. The first is engineered — specified, tested, monitored, budgeted. The second is emergent: accidental, untested, invisible. We propose degradation contracts: a declarative formalism (fidelity ladders per feature, safe shedding orders, hysteresis and recovery semantics, floor invariants) and a pressure broker that measures pressure, mediates directives, and audits compliance into a queryable ledger. The payoffs compound in the AI era, where quality is already a runtime variable negotiated invisibly; contracts make that negotiation inspectable and fair.

P-005

Provenance-Native Storage: The File System as a Causal Ledger

systemsstoragetrust

Every file is an amnesiac: it knows its size and mtime, and has forgotten who created it, from what inputs, over which network origin. PASS proved automatic file provenance feasible in 2006; nothing shipped. The barriers have expired: eBPF/EndpointSecurity/ETW capture substrates, content-addressed compaction, local analytical engines, and C2PA's market education. This paper specifies the causal event ledger, the application transform contract, the query algebra, and the retention semantics that treat forgetting as a feature — the trust infrastructure the AI era just made mandatory.

The Attention Scheduler: Interruption Economics as an Operating System Problem

OSHCI × systemsagents

OSes schedule processors, memory, and network with fifty years of theory; attention is scheduled by a DND toggle and an adversarial engagement economy at a measured cost of ~23 minutes of resumption per interruption, unrecorded. We specify the attention scheduler: interruption-cost models, admission control with deferral queues, breakpoint-timed dispatch, per-app budgets with honesty pricing, urgent-class bounded latency, starvation freedom, and an attention ledger. Closed platform features are approaching this system without the theory; the paper supplies the semantics first.

P-007

The Intermittent Compute Fabric: One Machine Across Many Devices

distributedlocal-firstAI infra

The median user owns five to eight devices that behave as islands joined only by cloud meters. The offloading research line died in 2012 for reasons that have quietly expired: mesh VPNs solved reachability, WASM and containers solved portability, device identity is solved, and distributed local AI is the first workload in a generation that needs cross-device placement. We specify the fabric: a scheduler that treats sleep and partition as inputs, a capability-delegation protocol, partition-tolerant task semantics, and an ambient CRDT state layer — one machine across many devices, owned.
